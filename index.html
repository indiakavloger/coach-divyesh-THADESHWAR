Body Mass Index (BMI)

Assess whether your body weight is in a healthy proportion to your height.

Daily Calorie & Macro Calculator

Calculate your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) and recommended daily calories using the Mifflin-St Jeor formula.

Hydration & Water Intake

Estimate the optimal daily fluid intake required for energetic metabolism and healthy digestion.

Ideal Body Weight Range

Determines your healthy weight bracket based on standard WHO BMI index (18.5 – 24.9) and the Devine medical formula.

Target heart-rate beats per minute (bpm) for warm-up, active fat oxidation, and cardiovascular fitness.

A proven clinical marker for visceral abdominal fat distribution and cardiovascular health indicators.
